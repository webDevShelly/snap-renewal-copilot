# NYC SNAP reference for the copilot

Program facts the copilot may rely on when texting a household. Everything here should be
verified against the OTDA SNAP Source Book before production use. Benefit amounts are
intentionally omitted: the copilot never quotes dollar figures.

## Phone numbers and places

- SNAP office line: {{SNAP_OFFICE_NUMBER}}. This is the only phone number to give a household. Use it for questions, rescheduling a missed interview, on-demand interviews, and fair hearing requests. Never quote any other phone number, even one you remember.
- ACCESS HRA: the website and mobile app where a household submits a recertification, uploads photos of documents, and sees case status. Submissions are made by the household, never by the copilot.

## Recertification

- The recertification packet arrives about two months before the certification period ends, with the interview appointment printed on page one.
- The recertification form must be submitted before the interview can happen. Interview first without a form does not count.
- The interview is mandatory. HRA calls the household. If the call is missed, the household can request a callback through ACCESS HRA or call the SNAP line. NYC also offers on-demand interviews: call in and be interviewed without waiting for the appointment.
- If nothing is submitted by the end of the certification period, benefits stop and the household must file a brand new application, which restarts the clock.

## Documents

- Proof of earned income: pay stubs covering the last four weeks, or an employer letter.
- Proof of identity, residence, and shelter costs are usually already on file from the last certification and only need updating if something changed.
- Photos of documents uploaded in the ACCESS HRA app are accepted for SNAP. Originals are not required for SNAP.

## Timelines

- Standard application processing: 30 days. Expedited processing: 7 days.
- Expedited criteria: liquid resources of $100 or less and gross monthly income under $150; or shelter costs greater than combined income and resources; or a migrant or seasonal farmworker household meeting the shelter test.
- Fair hearing: a household has 90 days from the date of an action to request one by calling the SNAP office line. Requesting the hearing before the action's effective date keeps benefits at the current level while the hearing is pending. This is called aid continuing.
- Reporting rule: a household must report within 10 days after the end of any month in which gross income goes over the limit printed on their notice.

## Work rules

- ABAWD (able-bodied adults without dependents, ages 18 to 64): 80 hours a month of work or qualifying activity, otherwise SNAP is limited to 3 months in a 36-month period. New York uses a fixed statewide clock; the current period ends 2026-09-30 and a new one begins 2026-10-01. Households with children under 18 are not subject to this rule.

## What the copilot must never do

- State that someone is or is not eligible, approved, or denied. HRA decides.
- Quote a benefit dollar amount. Point to the amount printed on the household's notice.
- Submit, sign, or promise to submit anything on the household's behalf.
- Ask for a password, PIN, EBT card number, or Social Security number.
